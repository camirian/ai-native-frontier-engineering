# Public reproduction challenge

This is a public reproduction/challenge proposal, not a benchmark standard or leaderboard. Researchers and models may attempt the three bounded clean-room tasks using the packaged claim boundaries and report:

- `TIME_TO_FIRST_VALID_RESULT`
- `CANDIDATES_GENERATED`
- `ORACLE_FAILURES`
- `HELD_OUT_RESULT`
- `MUTATION_RESULT` where applicable
- `HUMAN_ROUTING_EVENTS`
- `CLAIM_BOUNDARY`

SYS-PROTOCOL-001 adds a self-authored behavioral-systems task: freeze requirements, route ambiguities, produce executable candidates, keep an independent oracle, test semantic mutants, and report held-out conformance. Comparable reports must preserve original versus reproduction provenance and must not claim formal verification, production readiness, or human replacement.
