# Public reproduction challenge

This is a public reproduction/challenge proposal, not a benchmark standard or leaderboard. Reports should preserve `TIME_TO_FIRST_VALID_RESULT`, `CANDIDATES_GENERATED`, `ORACLE_FAILURES`, `HELD_OUT_RESULT`, `MUTATION_RESULT` where applicable, `HUMAN_ROUTING_EVENTS`, and `CLAIM_BOUNDARY`.

SYS-PROTOCOL-001 adds a self-authored behavioral-systems task. Reports must preserve original versus reproduction provenance and must not claim formal verification, production readiness, or human replacement.
