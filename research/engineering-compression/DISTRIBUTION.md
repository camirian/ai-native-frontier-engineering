# Distribution copy — engineering compression

## LinkedIn

AI made the implementation phase faster. It did not make the evidence phase optional.

In a bounded multidisciplinary engineering experiment, AI-assisted execution produced an inspectable simulator, controller contract, fault handling, deterministic replay, and independent evaluation across 30 unseen cases.

The result was `PARTIAL`, not a victory lap. A physically infeasible sustained-load corner remained. One implementation defect was found and repaired. No claim of team replacement, production safety, universal productivity, or “3600x” belongs here.

The useful finding is narrower: when generation gets cheaper, specifications, independent oracles, held-out tests, and claim discipline become the scarce engineering work.

Read the case study: https://github.com/camirian/ai-native-frontier-engineering/tree/main/research/engineering-compression
